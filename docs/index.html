Yearly Grid

Your whole Google Calendar year on one 12 × 31 grid — every month a row, every day a column — so trips, work blocks and busy stretches are visible at a glance.

  • Year at a glance. Multi-day and cross-month events stay on one line.
  • Choose your calendars. Show or hide any calendar in your Google account.
  • Edit in place. Click or drag across days to create an event; click one to change or delete it. Rest the mouse on an event to see its details.
  • Make it yours. Light or dark theme, accent colour, font, text size, and a filter to hide short timed events.
  • Private by design. No server: the extension talks only to Google. See the privacy policy.
